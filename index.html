Worldfitnow

Worldfitnow is a unique health & fitness app that allows customers to plot their cardio progress towards circumnavigating iconic landmarks around the world.The Worldfitnow app sets out to solve a problem of inspiring people to engage directly or indirectly in a health & fitness goal. This is achieved by the customer logging their time and distance their cardio activity towards circumnavigating an iconic landmark around the world, without actually having to be present at the landmark. Each landmark has a designated start and finish point. The customer simply selects a country on the app, a landmark, then a cardio activity assigned to that landmark. The customer then sets about completing their cardio activity. On the conclusion of each session, the distance and time taken to complete the session is logged. The app updates the customer’s progress towards completion of the landmark challenge.
